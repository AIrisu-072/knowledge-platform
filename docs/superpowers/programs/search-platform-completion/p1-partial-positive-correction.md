# P1 exact-text predicate — Partial positive correction

- Binding correction to `p1-body-absence-amendment.md` §3 and the composed P1 resolver/assembler contract; all other proof and current-authority requirements remain.
- `document.body.contains_exact` is true for the specified current Live parent Version iff the normalized nonempty literal occurs contiguously within one verified Unit from an authoritative item in `Completed + Supported` **or `Completed + Partial`**. The exact raw/locator/text/profile/parent/ClaimId bindings and current Source-owned Read/Version/Part checks must all pass. Unsupported/Failed/Retryable items cannot furnish Units or claims.
- A verified Partial Unit may produce the exact `Extracted` positive claim and qualified body hit. It does not prove completeness of the item or corpus: retain its access-filtered blocking coverage gap and keep global completion false. No unverified omission is treated as searched.
- Negative `ProvenAbsent` remains permitted only if every relevant current visible authoritative item is `Completed + Supported`, all Units and literal scans are complete under the pinned bundle and finite budget, and final current authority/bindings still match. Any Partial item makes the negative proof Unknown, including a Partial item whose returned Units have no matching literal.
- Acceptance counterexample: a Partial item has verified Unit `東京`, other Supported Units do not. Correct positive resolver yields the parent-bound exact claim plus blocking coverage gap; a negative scan for `大阪` over that same parent yields Unknown. A mismatched parent/locator/raw/Read never produces either claim.
- This selects the positive semantics already present in the original/revised P1 body design; it does not change S1, negative proof, or Source authority. No code/CI has been run for this document.
